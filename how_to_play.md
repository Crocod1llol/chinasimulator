# chinasimulator Alpha 2.0 guide

So.. you have found yourself in "china" town! (Not to be confused with other stuff)

You are a fat chinese, so you will die of hunger in 18 mins~ from 100/100 hunger.

**ITEMS AND CONTAINERS:**
You click on items in your inventory to interact with them. Some items can't be interacted with, for example: the fries you serve at your job.

You can press E on the chest in your house to open a GUI, there you can click on items from inventory to transfer them
to the container, or the opposite, click on items from the container to transfer them to inventory.

**DOORS:**

If you aren't blind, you probably observed the door in your house, you can press E on it to go outside.
From there, you can go to the supermarket, or to wendonalds (your working place)

**NPCS**

There are 2 types of NPCS:

--Predefined ones;

They are NPCS that either sell you something or are just there to talk with you. These NPCS cannot be killed.

--Randomly Spawned ones:

You will see these other fat chinese people. An npc will start every 2 minutes and at the start of the game, one npc will spawn.
They are harmless and they can be killed with a nuclear bomb (item id 4).

**SUPERMARKET:**

Its the main place where you would buy food and other stuff to help yourself. You mainly buy these things from the vending machine.

You would also see the cashier in the corner, he is harmless and only there to supervise the store.

YOu would interact with the vending machine like any container, but you would see a price tag on the items. 
You can only get items from the vending machine and not deposit there.

The vending machine restocks every 10 minutes.

**wendonalds:**

The working place where you would spend your time in. You get fries from the food cooker (the "box" with 'Grab food here') and
serve them at the counter ('Place food here'). By doing this procedure, you would get 2 cash every serving.

You would interact with the food cooker to turn it on, and after 10 seconds, the food will be done and you will have to interact with it again to grab the fries
(it also makes a ding sound when its done) and then you would grab the fries in your inventory (make sure you have space)

Then with the fries in the inventory, you will interact with the counter and serve the fries.

**china temple**

The place where *king jing um* stays, as he is visiting china.
He will sell you a nuclear bomb for 200 cash, and with that nuclear bomb you can blow up npcs.
